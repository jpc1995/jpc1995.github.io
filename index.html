● Accepted · LACEA-LAMES 2026

Who Benefits from Generative AI? Wage Evidence from Peru

Jorge Peña

Presented at the XVIII Jornadas de Economía Laboral (AEET), Málaga, 2026. Uses ENAHO 2024 microdata, the GBB occupational exposure index, and a Heckman selection model with Bartik shift-share IV to estimate the wage premium associated with GenAI exposure among formal urban workers in Peru.

[SSRN — coming July 2026]

● Work in Progress

GenAI Occupational Exposure in Peru: A Dynamic Index (GBB 2023–2025)

Jorge Peña, Vicente Corzo Valdiglesias, Juan Manuel García Carpio

Constructs a delta index of GenAI occupational exposure using EPEN + GBB waves, targeting LACEA and APE 2026.

● Doctoral Proposal

Dynamic Measurement of GenAI Occupational Exposure in Latin America

Jorge Peña

Applies Functional Data Analysis (FDA) and functional time series methods to track the evolution of occupational exposure to generative AI across 6+ Latin American countries. DCIES Doctoral Dissertation, UNI-FIEECS.

● Institutional Report

Sectoral Employment-Output Elasticities and Formal Employment Projections 2026–2027

DISEL Research Team – MTPE Peru

Boletín de Economía Laboral. Driscoll-Kraay Fixed Effects panel models and VAR/TAR on Planilla Electrónica data. 8 sectors, 24 departments, 18-year panel.
